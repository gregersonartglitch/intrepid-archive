# The Hollowlands — World Overview

## What It Is
The Hollowlands is a fantasy world that exists as one half of a reality split by the Maxim Stone. The other half is called **the Clockwork**. The two halves are held apart by the Maxim Stone — a monolith no larger than a person that acts as a suture over a wound the size of everything.

## The Split
The Maxim Stone does not separate the worlds. It is the wound's suture. Remove it and the wound reopens. Elena's first journey ended at the Maxim Stone. This is where the prophecy began to prove itself.

## The Fog
The Hollowlands is shrouded in a supernatural fog. The map represents a cartographer's record of what has been verified, discovered, and witnessed. Locations exist in the fog — players (and Elena) must find them.

## The Tetrad
The ruling religious/political authority. They cast nine old gods into Kur (the underworld/void) and built the Tower of Nine to cage them. The ceremonies at the Tower are not worship — they are lockpicking.

## The Nine (Old Gods)
Nine old gods banished by the Tetrad. The Tower of Nine has one story for each. The statues inside the Tower face the Maxim Stone — not outward. They are in attitudes of judgment.

## The Prophecy (Isin Ada's Oracle)
*"Three will rise. One Dusk, One Dark, One Dawn destroying Stone. Ushering the end."*
Isin Ada spoke this once and then wept for six days. Elena received this prophecy at the Monastery of the Wind and did not want it.

## The Dilmun Ruins
Shattered remnants of the world that existed before the Maxim Stone split reality. Paradoxical — appear ancient and freshly destroyed simultaneously. Flowers grow here in colors the eye processes as grief. Standing in the ruins, one can feel the phantom weight of the Clockwork — the other half of the world, pressing close, remembering.

## Geography Notes
The Hollowlands contains:
- **17 territories** (regions and water bodies)
- **13 cartographer sites** (verified cities, sacred places, ancient sites)
- **8 journey stops** along Elena's path through Volume 1
- Multiple factions, sacred sites, ruins, and mysteries beyond Volume 1

## The Clockwork
The other half of the split reality. Elena came FROM the Clockwork — she is not native to the Hollowlands. The Crossing Pool is where she arrived.
