# Characters — The Hollowlands

## Elena
**Role:** Protagonist. Volume 1 follows her first journey through the Hollowlands.  
**Origin:** From the Clockwork (the other half of the split reality). Not native to the Hollowlands.  
**Arrival:** Surfaced through the Crossing Pool — gasping, disoriented, in a world that was not hers yet recognized her with the familiarity of a mirror.  
**Arc:** Found the Dawn Spear (it permitted itself to be found). Sought her grandmother Sabella. Received the Oracle's prophecy. Reached the Maxim Stone where her first journey ended.  
**Key trait:** The Hollowlands recognized her before she recognized it.

---

## Sabella (The Moon Queen)
**Role:** Elena's grandmother. Former DOD remote viewer. Now rules the last free territory in the Hollowlands as the Moon Queen.  
**Origin:** Crossed from the Clockwork carrying only grief and a government-issued sidearm. Built a kingdom from one and buried the other. (Which was which depends on who tells the story.)  
**The Stronghold:** Silver-walled fortress. Gleams with light that has no visible source. Corridors rearrange according to the Moon Queen's mood.  
**Key fact:** Sabella's Hut — her original shelter — is larger inside than outside. She left behind a DOD field manual adapted into a gardening guide, star charts relabeled with Hollowlands constellations, and an unfinished letter addressed to someone named Nick.  
**Key quote:** *"Sabella crossed from the Clockwork carrying only grief and a government-issued sidearm. She built a kingdom from one and buried the other."*

---

## Isin Ada
**Role:** The Oracle. Resides at the Monastery of the Wind.  
**Method:** Reads the future in the patterns of dust storms.  
**The prophecy:** Spoke *"Three will rise. One Dusk, One Dark, One Dawn destroying Stone. Ushering the end."* — then wept for six days.  
**The Monastery:** Accessible only by a path that the wind erases nightly. Pilgrims must find a new route each dawn.

---

## Nick
**Who:** Mentioned only in Sabella's unfinished letter found at Sabella's Hut. Identity unknown. Significant enough that Sabella was writing to them across worlds.

---

## Writing Voice Notes
- The world has a quiet, melancholy weight. Lore reads like archaeological footnotes.
- Quotes are in the form of remembered sayings, inscriptions, or things someone said once.
- Elena's experience is one of recognition — the world knows her even when she doesn't know it.
- Sabella's backstory is a study in grief becoming power.
- Nothing is explained fully. The best lore raises more questions than it answers.
