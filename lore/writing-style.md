# Writing Style Guide — The Hollowlands

## Voice & Tone
The world has a quiet, melancholy weight. Lore reads like **archaeological footnotes** — things someone recorded because they felt it mattered, not because they needed to explain it.

## Location Description Formula
Each location description should:
1. **Establish the physical** — what does it look, feel, smell, sound like?
2. **Embed the uncanny** — one detail that makes it subtly wrong or impossibly right
3. **Root it in Elena or Sabella's experience** — what happened here, what did they feel?
4. **End with consequence** — why does this place matter to the larger story?

## Lore Quote Formula
Lore quotes are presented as remembered sayings, inscriptions, or things someone said once:
- Usually 1-3 sentences
- Begin with a paradox or reframe of the obvious
- Never fully explain — the best lore raises more questions
- Format: `"Quote text."`

## Examples of Good Voice

**Good:**  
*"A still, black pool of water no wider than a room, ringed by stones that bear the scorch marks of repeated transit."*

**Good:**  
*"The Spear was not forged. The Spear was promised."*

**Bad (too explanatory):**  
*"This is where the magic pool is that lets people travel between worlds."*

**Bad (too modern):**  
*"A portal to another dimension."*

## Key Thematic Anchors
- **Recognition without knowledge** — the world knows Elena before she knows it
- **Grief as architecture** — characters build their lives and kingdoms from loss
- **The wound metaphor** — the split between worlds is always a wound, never just a gap
- **Memory and stone** — physical places remember. The stone remembers.
- **Prophecy as burden** — foreknowledge is not power, it is obligation

## Names & Proper Nouns
- **The Hollowlands** — always capitalized, always "The"
- **the Clockwork** — lowercase "the", capitalized "Clockwork"  
- **the Tetrad** — lowercase "the"
- **Kur** — the underworld/void where the Nine were banished
- **Matgul** — a forested region (canopy described as "dense")
- **OG** — a region bordering Matgul
- **Isin Ada** — the Oracle, female pronouns
- **Elena** — protagonist, female pronouns
- **Sabella / the Moon Queen** — same person, female pronouns

## What NOT To Do
- Don't use modern/tech language unless deliberately contrasting (Sabella's DOD background is one exception)
- Don't over-explain the magic system — it works because it works
- Don't add new named characters without checking with the author first
- Don't change existing lore text in data.js without explicit instruction
